# ~/.config (a Hyprland rice with swappable setups)

Before any work on the desktop — a new setup, a new colour theme, the bar/panels, the theme tool,
wallpapers, the lock screen — read `theme/SETUPS.md` in full. It is the playbook: what a setup is,
what every setup must provide, the procedure for making a new one, and the known gotchas.

Asked to "make another setup": follow SETUPS.md §7 step by step, from the decision in step 0 to
the verification in step 5 and the commit and push in step 6.
